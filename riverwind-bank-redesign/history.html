Our history

A long local story, moving forward.

RiverWind Bank was formerly The Bank of Augusta, chartered in 1935. The bank expanded to Searcy and later Beebe while keeping its community-first approach.

1935

The beginning

The Bank of Augusta opened its doors on February 20 during the Great Depression, receiving more than $120,000 in deposits on its first day.

1996

Growing locally

A second Augusta location opened on Highway 64, including the bank's first ATM.

2012–2015

Into White County

The bank opened locations in Searcy, extending its footprint beyond Woodruff County.

2022

Beebe

RiverWind expanded again into Beebe, a natural fit because of existing customer relationships in the market.

RiverWind.Bank

Community banking with a modern breeze. Personal service, practical products, and technology that makes everyday banking easier.
