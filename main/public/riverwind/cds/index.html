Traditional IRA

Tax-deferred retirement savings

Traditional IRAs use before-tax earnings and generally defer taxes until qualified withdrawals.

Contributions

Contribution limits and eligibility are governed by IRS rules and can change by tax year.

Withdrawals & penalties

RiverWind's published information describes qualified withdrawals and exceptions to the 10% early-withdrawal penalty. Confirm current rules with a tax professional.

Roth IRA

Tax-free qualified withdrawals

Roth IRAs are funded with after-tax earnings, with qualified withdrawals generally tax-free.

Contributions

Eligibility and annual contribution limits depend on IRS rules and adjusted gross income.

Withdrawals & penalties

Qualified withdrawals can be tax-free; early distributions may be subject to taxes or penalties depending on circumstances.
