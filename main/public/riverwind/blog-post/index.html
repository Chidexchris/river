Summer Savings

June 10, 2026 • Banking • Savings • Spending

Summer can bring higher gas, grocery, travel and utility spending. A little planning can make the season easier on your budget.

Start with a seasonal budget

Look at recurring expenses, expected travel and one-time activities before the season gets busy. Give each category a simple ceiling and check progress weekly.

Make saving automatic

Small automatic transfers can create a buffer without requiring a new decision every payday.

Leave room for the unexpected

A practical cushion can keep an unexpected bill from becoming a credit-card balance.
